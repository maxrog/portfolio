Hello, I’m Max Rogers! I’m a passionate mobile developer, specifically iOS. Below is a recap of my work experience. You can also go back to my website’s home page and swipe to see more about some of the apps I’ve built.

My Experience at Hivecast • December 2017

Hivecast is a startup company centered around a livestreaming application. The CEO, Paul L and his partner Ryan D , are two very driven individuals and a pleasure to work with. They made sure to include me in the whole process, listening to my ideas and input. I learned some of the ins and outs, and struggles to create a successful product from scratch. It takes a lot more work than just developing some software. The iOS application offers a unique twist on livestreaming, displaying multiple streams at once with one user as the director. It had a complex network layer that I enjoyed investigating and required some thoughtful debugging. The app is still in its early stages but I gained experience on picking up a foreign codebase and contributing as soon as possible. I also encountered hurdles to be overcome working and managing the development lifecyle remotely.

My Experience at the App Factory • December 2016

The App Factory is an interdisciplinary group located on UW-Parkside’s campus. We are made up of faculty mentors and student interns who develop iOS, Android, and web applications.

  • We abide by Agile Methodologies, perform weekly standups, make use of Jira for project management, Slack for communication, and rotate Scrum roles for new projects.
  • Since we work with real clients, I’ve learned how to communicate ideas to technical and non-techinal people alike.
  • I’ve experienced all the small details that go into quality products. Including writing Statements of Work, UI/UX Design, Testing, Demoing properly, apt presentation of applications on the App Store and more. The little things are what set applications apart and make for a happy client.
  • I’ve dealt with the pressure to deliver a product in time for a hard deadline.
  • Even just hanging around with other aspiring professionals, whether it’s learning something new or tutoring a new recruit, has molded me into a more well-rounded developer.

I’ve had a blast working in this environment and it’s given me the drive to never stop learning!
