Dewey's Racine

Restaurant & Sports Bar

Freelance Project
Client: Dewey’s Restaurant & Sports Bar
Initial Release: March 2018
iOS Team Size: 1

Contributions & Takeaways

The Dewey’s app is a tool for the business owners to keep customers up to date on their events and general information about the establishment.
Takeaways

  • Firebase CMS Used to send push notifications to users about daily specials etc.
  • Google Calendar Their Google Calendar data is pulled and filtered to display data.
  • Unit Testing Implement unit testing for Realm classes, network, manager classes, and view controllers.

This app is simple at it’s core.
I was given permission from the client to make the code available on GitHub.
